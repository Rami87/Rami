Prices you know beforehand

No price riddle: you know before every job what it costs. All figures are net, plus VAT.

The start

  • First call: freeAbout 20 minutes. We listen and clarify what matters in your business. No obligation.
  • IT check: €99 excl. VATReport with a traffic light per area. If you commission a project or a maintenance contract, the amount is credited in full. More about the IT check

What determines the price

Every business is different. That is why we charge by scope, not by a fixed formula.

  • Number of devices and users
  • Desired response time
  • On site or by remote support
  • Existing technology that can continue to be used
  • One-off project or ongoing care

Ongoing care: HORANiQ Care

More about Care. Planned instead of only when something is broken.

  • Care StartFor very small businesses: regular checks and remote support.
  • Care BusinessFor growing businesses: plus backup monitoring, updates and Microsoft 365 care.
  • Care ProFor more devices and users: priority support and regular reports.
  • Care PraxisFor practices: short response times, closer backup monitoring and detailed documentation.

You receive monthly packages as a written quote after the first call.

Questions about prices
