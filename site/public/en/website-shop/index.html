Care after the launch

Maintenance, updates and content changes are possible by agreement. What is included and whether it is commissioned individually or as part of an ongoing agreement is set out in the quote.

Not automatically part of a website or shop project are, for example, texts and photos, hosting, advertising, ongoing search engine optimisation, translations and payment and shipping integrations. What you need and what we take over, we clarify before the project starts.

We promise no particular Google ranking and no sales figures. We make sure your site is clearly structured, easy to use and technically clean.
