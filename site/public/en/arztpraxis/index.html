Frequently asked questions

Do you also look after our practice software and e-card connection?

We look after the IT infrastructure of your practice. For questions about practice software or the e-card connection we coordinate with the responsible provider. We clarify responsibilities before we begin.

Do I have to change all my IT?

No. We first check what is there and improve only what is necessary.

Do you also work on site?

Yes, in Vienna and the surrounding area, up to about an hour's travel. Much can also be solved by remote support.

How quickly do I get help?

We get back to your request promptly. For ongoing care we agree support and response times together with you.

What does the care cost?

Your quote depends on devices, users and the agreed service hours. After the first call you receive a clear quote. All prices are net of VAT.

How do you handle sensitive data?

We treat your data discreetly and set up access only as far as needed for the work. We clarify legal agreements such as data processing with you before we begin.

Do you also look after dental and therapy practices?

Yes, as well as group practices and other medical facilities.

Do you speak English and Arabic?

Yes, we are happy to support you in German, English and Arabic. Contracts and invoices are in German.
