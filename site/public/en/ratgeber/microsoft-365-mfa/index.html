Securing Microsoft 365: MFA explained simply

A stolen password is often enough for attackers. A second factor makes access much harder.

What is MFA?

MFA means that signing in needs something second in addition to the password, usually a confirmation in an app on your phone. Whoever knows only the password cannot get in.

Why does it matter?

Email accounts often contain contracts, invoices and confidential messages. A taken-over account can also be used to send messages in your name.

How to introduce it in your business

  • Start with administrators and management, then the whole team.
  • Use an authenticator app instead of SMS alone, where possible.
  • Schedule a short appointment where everyone sets up the app together.
  • Decide what happens if someone loses their phone.

Common concerns

The effort in daily life is small: the confirmation is usually only requested on a new device or location. We are happy to set up MFA together with you. More: Microsoft 365 in your business.
