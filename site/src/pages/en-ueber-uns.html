Rami Horani, founder

My career began with studying information technology and networks at the University of Damascus. I then worked in the technical environment of the University of Damascus and looked after extensive IT infrastructures in continuous operation, focusing on networks, IT security, device management and technical fault analysis.

After that I spent around eight years at one of the largest technology and e-commerce companies in the world. There I combined technical responsibility with structured troubleshooting, process optimisation and reliable solutions for daily operations.

With HORANiQ I now bring this technical experience directly to businesses – personal, understandable and solution-oriented.
