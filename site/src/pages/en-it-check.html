When the check is worthwhile

The IT check is a good start if you want to know where your IT stands overall, for example before an investment or before you agree on ongoing care.

You do not need it for individual faults or simple support. It is also not necessary for a website or shop project.

The check is a first overview and not a comprehensive security audit. It is no guarantee for the security of your systems.

The result can lead to a project or ongoing care with HORANiQ Care. You decide whether and what to implement.

Frequently asked questions

What does the IT check cost?

The first call is free. After that you receive a clear quote, at a fixed price where possible. All prices are net.

Is the IT check a security audit?

No. It is a first overview of your IT with priorities. It is not a comprehensive security audit.

Do I need the IT check for every request?

No. It is not needed for individual faults, simple support and website and shop projects.

Who is the IT check meant for?

For businesses that want to know where their IT stands overall, for example medical practices and offices and businesses.

Where do you work?

In Vienna and the surrounding area, up to about an hour's travel.

Do you speak English and Arabic?

Yes, we are happy to support you in German, English and Arabic. Contracts and invoices are in German.
