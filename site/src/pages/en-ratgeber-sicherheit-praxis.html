IT security in the medical practice: a checklist to tick off

Patient data is sensitive. This list shows what you can check yourself without technical knowledge and where a second look makes sense.

Access and sign-in

  • Does every person at reception and in the team have an own account instead of a shared password?
  • Is a second factor (MFA) active for email and cloud services?
  • Are accounts of people who leave blocked immediately?

Devices and updates

  • Are Windows and practice software updated regularly?
  • Are workstations locked when unattended, especially in rooms with patient traffic?
  • Is there an up-to-date list of all devices, including printers and routers?

Network and Wi-Fi

  • Are the patient Wi-Fi and the practice network separate?
  • Is the firewall up to date and maintained?
  • Is the network cabinet or router kept out of everyone's reach?

Data backup

  • Does the backup run automatically and does someone check that it succeeded?
  • Has a recovery ever really been tested? How to test it
  • Is there a copy in a different place from the original data?

What next?

Every point you answer with no or are not sure about deserves a closer look. The IT check examines these areas and rates them with a traffic light: OK, improvement recommended or critical. For questions about data protection duties in your practice, please also consult your data protection or legal adviser. This list does not replace a legal review.
